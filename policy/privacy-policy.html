04 Copyright & Intellectual Property

NOXHUB respects the rights of copyright owners and expects users and content providers to comply with applicable intellectual property laws.

Nothing in this policy authorizes unauthorized copying, distribution, public communication, or circumvention of technical access restrictions.

If you believe that material accessible through our website infringes your copyright or other intellectual property rights, please contact us with the information requested in our content removal procedure.

We will review complaints in good faith and take appropriate steps where warranted by the available information and applicable law. These steps may include disabling access to a link, removing material under our control, or contacting the relevant third-party provider.

05 Copyright Complaints & Removal

Copyright owners or their authorized representatives may submit a complaint by email. Please include the following details to help us review the request:

  • Your name and contact information.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work.
  • The exact URL on NOXHUB where the material appears.
  • The external content URL, if available.
  • Evidence of ownership or authorization to represent the owner.
  • A description of the alleged infringement and a statement that the information supplied is accurate to the best of your knowledge.

Send copyright and content removal requests to: noxhub.in@gmail.com

We will review sufficiently detailed complaints and respond within a reasonable period, subject to applicable law and the information provided. Additional details may be requested when necessary.

Where appropriate, access may be restricted while a complaint is reviewed. If a file is controlled by a third-party provider, the complaint may also need to be submitted to that provider.

A complaint does not automatically establish infringement. We assess requests based on the information available and applicable requirements.
